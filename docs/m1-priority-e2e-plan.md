2. **`testGenericScrollCancelsPendingRestoration`** (<=75 seconds): reuse existing debug `NEOMD_UI_TEST_RESIZE_RESTORATION_DELAY_MILLISECONDS=1500`; at middle record semantic point, resize height by 20 pt, then send one upward generic scroll while restoration is pending. 3. **Physical trackpad check — user-owned, not an unattended XCTest pass:** same proven product/window/8-copy corpus at fifth copy, no active restoration/find/selection. Record user/device confirmation, natural-scroll setting without changing it, and whether any unrelated gesture occurred.
   Require travel continues after finger lift while momentum deltas continue; no interior abrupt freeze/jump or unintended correction; record boundary distance to distinguish legitimate top contact.
